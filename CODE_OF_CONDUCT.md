# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project should be a respectful, useful place to collaborate. Contributors are expected to be clear, kind, constructive, and focused on improving the work.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- giving specific technical feedback;
- asking questions when context is missing;
- crediting others' ideas;
- accepting correction gracefully;
- prioritizing reproducibility, fairness, and user safety.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language;
- publishing private information without permission;
- intentionally misleading users about model quality or limitations;
- repeated off-topic disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors. Reports should include enough context to understand what happened and will be reviewed with care.
